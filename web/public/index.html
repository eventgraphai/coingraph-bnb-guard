How it works

Ask before the trade

The agent sends the token and the order size.

Check on BNB Chain

A real buy and sell is simulated on PancakeSwap, plus liquidity, owner powers and price impact.

Cross-check the data

CoinMarketCap confirms the token's identity and market price. Atlas Oracle gives a signed reference price.

Sign, record, enforce

The answer is signed and its hash recorded on opBNB. The guard contract only lets a "proceed" trade through.
